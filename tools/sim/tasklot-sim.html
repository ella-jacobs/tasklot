TaskLot on the bench

Order at the speaker, park to end the order, move cars to re-rank, drive into the pickup lane when it's done. The whole interaction with no hardware plugged in — and every line in the log is exactly what the board prints over serial, so what you rehearse here is what the board does.

01The lot

You order a task the way you order a burger. Drive a blank car past the speaker and say what you need to do — it starts listening. Then park it: coming to rest is what ends the order, so there is no stop button. The spot you pick is the priority — space 1 is next up — and moving a car re-ranks the list. Bring a car that already has a task back to the speaker and it reads it back to you in full and names the first move — same as a real speaker, which takes your order and then repeats it. When the task is done, drive into the pickup lane and the car goes blank, ready to order again.

02What the speaker heard

This panel is the laptop's half of an order — you never touch it during the interaction. Driving a car past the speaker starts the mic; parking the car ends it and binds what was heard. The long version stays on this page, an 18-character version goes to the screen. Typing is the offline fallback: type while a car is at the speaker and parking binds that instead.
